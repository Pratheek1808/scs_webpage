Payroll Management System

One upload. Full compliance. Automated payroll processing with EPF, ESI, PT and TDS computation — plus instant generation of 20+ statutory labour-law forms.

How It Works

1

Upload

Fill in the provided Excel template with employee master data, wage details, attendance, and leave records — then upload it to the system.

2

Compute

The engine automatically calculates Basic + DA, EPF, ESI, PT, TDS and net salary for every employee based on statutory rules.

3

Download

Get a ZIP of 20+ pre-filled statutory forms — wage registers, wage slips, muster rolls, annual returns, gratuity nominations and more.

What's Included

A complete payroll and compliance suite built around Indian statutory requirements.

Payroll Computation Engine

  • ✓ Basic + DA at 51% of CTC
  • ✓ EPF Employee (12%, capped ₹1,800) & Employer (13%)
  • ✓ ESI Employee (0.75%) & Employer (3.25%) for wages < ₹21,000
  • ✓ Professional Tax slab-based (₹0 / ₹150 / ₹200)
  • ✓ TDS at 10% of Basic+DA if annual Basic+DA > ₹4,00,000
  • ✓ Downloadable Payroll Register (live Excel formulas)

Statutory Form Generation

  • ✓ Form I — Wage Register (Wages Act)
  • ✓ Form IV — Employee Register
  • ✓ Form V — Wage Slips
  • ✓ Form VIII-A/B/D — OSH Muster Roll, Wage Register & Slips
  • ✓ Form IX / IX-A — Annual & Contractor Returns
  • ✓ Form XI — Leave Register, Form X — Gratuity Nominations

ESI Compliance Module

  • ✓ ESIC Monthly Contribution tracking per employee
  • ✓ IP Number, wages, days worked & reason codes
  • ✓ One-click ESIC Monthly Contribution Excel export
  • ✓ Auto-sync from payroll run

PF / EPF ECR Module

  • ✓ UAN-based ECR with EPF & EPS computation
  • ✓ EPF wage cap ₹15,000; EPS capped ₹1,250
  • ✓ Export as Excel, CSV or EPFO upload TXT
  • ✓ Auto-sync from payroll run
